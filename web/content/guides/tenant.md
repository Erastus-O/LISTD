Welcome. The tenant portal is where you see what's been agreed about the
property you live in. You'll spend most of your time on the **portal home**
(an overview of upcoming and recent inspections) and the **report view**
(reading and signing off the documents that affect your deposit).

## Signing in

You access the portal through a personal link from your letting agent or
landlord. Open the link on the same device you'll use day to day — the
session is tied to that device and your email. If the link has expired,
ask whoever sent it to re-send a fresh one; we don't issue tenant
passwords directly, so there's nothing to reset.

## What you can do

- **See upcoming inspections.** Date, time slot, and the type of
  inspection (inventory, check-in, mid-term, check-out, interim). If a
  date or time looks wrong, contact your agent before the clerk arrives.
- **Read your reports.** Once a clerk has completed an inspection and an
  admin has approved the official version, the report appears under
  **Documents**. You can read it in the browser or download the PDF.
- **Sign off a report.** When a check-in or check-out report is ready
  for your acknowledgement, you'll see a **Sign off** button on the
  report page. Read each room's notes and condition first — once you've
  signed off, the report counts as agreed for deposit purposes.
- **Raise a query.** Disagree with something? Use the **Raise a query**
  link on the report. It opens a short form that goes back to your
  agent and the LISTD team — explain the room, the item, and what you
  think should change.

## What you can't do

The tenant portal is intentionally read-only for most things. You
**can't** edit a report, change inspection dates, or message the clerk
directly. Anything that needs a change goes through your agent or
landlord — they have the controls on their side of the platform.

## Privacy and data

- The portal only shows reports for properties you live in (or recently
  lived in). You can't see other tenants' reports.
- Sign-off events are recorded with the date and the device you used.
  This is part of the audit trail that protects you in a deposit
  dispute.
- You can ask your agent to delete your account at the end of a
  tenancy. Reports tied to a finished tenancy may be kept by your agent
  for the legal retention period — the LISTD team can't override that.

## Getting help

If something doesn't add up in a report, **don't sign off** — raise a
query first. If you can't sign in at all, contact your agent so they
can re-send your portal link. For anything that looks like a security
issue (e.g. you're seeing a property that isn't yours), email the
LISTD team directly and we'll lock the access while we investigate.
