Maintenance

Cleaning and repairs have their own pricing

They never borrow inspection prices. You always see a fixed price before you pay.

Cleaning

See an estimate while you book, then approve a fixed quote for a stated checklist before you pay.

  • Standard Home Cleaning: worktops, hob, sink, bathrooms, floors, dusting and bins. Minimum booking applies.
  • Deep Cleaning: adds oven, fridge and freezer, inside cupboards, interior windows and behind furniture.
  • End of Tenancy Cleaning: to a check-out standard, after a condition and appliance assessment.
  • Office Cleaning: quoted on floor area, facilities, access and frequency.
  • Add-ons where not included: oven, fridge, inside cupboards, carpets, exterior windows.

Repairs & Maintenance

A fixed task price when the job is clear. A paid diagnosis when it isn’t.

  • Fixed task price: an itemised quote for labour and materials, approved before the repair is booked.
  • Diagnostic visit: finds the cause and prices the repair. The fee is credited against the repair if you go ahead.
  • Any additional work needs your approval before it starts.

Portfolio pricing

Pay per completed service, with no subscription. Regular, well-planned work earns better rates.

  • 1–9 jobs a month: standard prices.
  • 10–24 jobs a month: up to a 5% rebate on service fees.
  • 25+ jobs a month: a negotiated rate based on location, scope and scheduling.
  • Rebates exclude materials, exceptional travel and Priority premiums.

About LISTD

Controlled infrastructure, not gig-economy chaos

LISTD brings professional standards to UK property services. We connect letting agents and landlords with verified Pros for inventories, inspections, repairs and maintenance, and cleaning through one structured service standard: consistent quality, clear fixed prices and secure payment.
