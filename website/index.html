About LISTD

Controlled infrastructure, not gig-economy chaos

LISTD brings professional standards to UK property services. We connect letting agents and landlords with verified Pros for inventories, inspections and cleaning through a structured, tier-based system: consistent quality, fair pricing and secure payment.

What we stand for

Verified professionals

Every Pro passes identity verification, qualification checks and a probationary period before accessing live jobs.

Escrow-secured payments

Client funds are held in escrow from job acceptance. Pros are guaranteed payment on approved delivery.

Fair dispute resolution

A structured process with admin oversight. Every party is heard, evidence is reviewed and resolutions are documented.

Full transparency

Every action is timestamped and tracked. Clients and Pros have complete visibility into job progress.

Strikes & accountability

Pros who fall below standards receive strikes with clear severity levels. Persistent issues lead to suspension.

Non-circumvention

All parties agree to operate within the platform, protecting the network and fair pay for everyone.
